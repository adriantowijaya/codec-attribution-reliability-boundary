# Third-Party Data

Raw audio is not redistributed in this private staging repository.

- Orchset raw audio is not redistributed and remains subject to upstream terms.
- IRMAS raw audio is not redistributed and remains subject to upstream terms.
- FSDnoisy18k raw audio is not redistributed and remains subject to upstream terms.
- LibriSpeech raw audio is not redistributed and remains subject to upstream terms.

Third-party material is referenced only through provenance and regeneration documentation. This repository does not re-license third-party corpora, third-party software, external codec implementations, or other third-party material.
